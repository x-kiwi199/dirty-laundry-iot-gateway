# Risk Assessment

## Technical Risks

- LTE instability during long-lived TLS sessions
- Silent connection drops without visible errors
- Power interruptions due to battery depletion
- Battery degradation from continuous float charging

## Usability Risks

- Manual activation forgotten before appliance start
- Solution too complex for non-technical users
- System perceived as unreliable or annoying or simply inconvenient

## Economic Risks

- Hardware cost exceeds perceived benefit
- Data costs exceed expectations
- "Smart" functionality becomes more expensive than savings

## Mitigation Strategies

- Prefer mature consumer devices with optimized power management
- Favor always-on simplicity over aggressive optimization
- Separate experimental setups from the production system

# Requirements

## Functional Requirements

- Appliances must reliably connect to the Miele cloud
- Smart features must work without manual intervention
- Long wash and dry cycles (up to ~4 hours) must complete without continous connectivity loss or dropped data reports
- Stable long-lived TLS connections to Miele servers

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Battery-powered operation (no free power outlets available)
- Recharge frequency no more than 1–2 times per month for convenience
- Minimal maintenance and configuration
- Usable by all household members without technical knowledge. A even better solution would be completetly transparent with no interactions by other household members whatsoever.

## Economic Requirements

- Predictable and low data costs
- Avoid expensive hardware with long break-even periods
- Monthly operating cost should be modest and justifiable
